# 0006 — Affiliate Neutrality

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The project may use comparison and affiliate links, but users must receive
recommendations based on product criteria rather than commission.

## Decision

Affiliate commission never influences recommendation ranking.

## Consequences

The product rule and commercial boundary are authoritative in [Project Charter](../governance/PROJECT_CHARTER.md).

## Reversal condition

Any affiliate-policy or ranking-incentive change requires explicit owner
approval as a commercial decision and a later approved Phase Brief.
