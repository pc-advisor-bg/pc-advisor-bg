# 0010 — €0 Mandatory Recurring-Cost Target

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The project must keep launch infrastructure affordable without silently trading
safety for a free-tier workaround.

## Decision

Target €0 mandatory recurring infrastructure cost at launch wherever reasonably
safe; no paid resource or automatic paid upgrade is agent-authorized.

## Consequences

Cost stops and owner approval are authoritative in [Stop Conditions](../governance/STOP_CONDITIONS.md).

## Reversal condition

Any non-zero recurring cost, paid add-on, or automatic paid upgrade requires
explicit owner approval before activation.
