# Project Charter

## Purpose

PC Advisor BG is an owner-controlled, agent-executed Bulgarian independent PC advisor and guided configurator.

This charter protects the approved V1 product boundary while the project operating system is installed.

## Readers and use

| Reader | When | Enables |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Reviewing phase intent or a stop | Product and boundary decisions |
| Agent | Planning or assessing scope | Approved work selection |
| Reviewer | Reviewing a task or phase | Scope conformance check |

## Product boundary

V1 serves Bulgarian users choosing a new PC tower or components within a €600–€4,000 budget.

The recommendation is performance-first, with hard quality and compatibility floors.

The primary build stays within budget.

Optional Save Money and Best Value builds may be offered under the approved product rules.

Best Value may exceed the budget by at most 7% only when a meaningful gain exists.

Recommendations use exact component models, up to five games or Mixed, and up to two additional workloads.

CPU and GPU choices are brand-neutral.

Pazaruvaj is the primary comparison call to action; direct affiliate links may appear where available.

Affiliate commission never influences recommendation ranking.

Benchmark evidence is separate from ranking logic.

Hardware selection is deterministic: V1 uses no external AI and no AI runtime dependency.

V1 requires no public user accounts; Admin authentication is for one owner account.

Shared result snapshots remain stable over time.

## Резюме за собственика

V1 е независим български помощник за избор на нови PC компоненти в рамките на €600–€4,000, с ясни и детерминистични препоръки.

Не добавяме marketplace, плащания, публични акаунти, външен AI или несвързана инфраструктура; подробният списък е по-долу.

Ти решаваш за пари, акаунти и възстановяване, правни и търговски въпроси, Production и обхвата на фазите.

## Explicit V1 exclusions

The following are out of scope unless a later owner-approved Phase Brief changes the boundary:

- Marketplace functionality or used hardware.
- Checkout or internal payments.
- Forums, community systems, or public user accounts.
- AI hardware selection or an external AI runtime.
- Unauthorized automated scraping.
- Microservices, Redis, vector databases, or realtime infrastructure.
- General-purpose CMS architecture or a mobile app.
- Mass autogenerated SEO pages or news-site functionality.
- Unrelated refactors or infrastructure modernization.

## Product and ownership principles

The owner controls accounts, recovery, money, legal and commercial decisions, Production authorization, and product or phase scope.

Inside an approved Phase Brief, the agent controls the technical path, tests, diagnostics, repairs, branches, commits, pull requests, reviews, and Staging verification.

The repository is public source, not open source. No open-source license or external-contribution program is granted for V1.

Everything committed must be safe to publish. Use symbolic placeholders such as `PROJECT_OWNER_EMAIL`; never commit real identifiers, credentials, recovery material, backups, or private operational data.

Routine owner operations should eventually be owner-friendly and avoid requiring SQL, source edits, environment changes, or GitHub operations where appropriate.

## Authority boundaries

A Phase Brief is the work authorization package. Discovery, chat history, dashboards, comments, old plans, and roadmap items are context only, not authorization.

Future ideas belong in the private Project Parking Lot and remain inactive until a later approved phase authorizes them.

The active phase does not authorize the next phase. In particular, Phase 4 is not authorized by Phase 3.

For agent authority and stop classifications, see [AGENT_AUTONOMY.md](AGENT_AUTONOMY.md) and [STOP_CONDITIONS.md](STOP_CONDITIONS.md).
